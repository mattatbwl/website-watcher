Research
Explore this Section
The Center for Housing Research and Innovative Solutions advances
rigorous, independent, data-driven research to inform housing policy and practice across Colorado.
Working at the intersection of law, real estate, public policy, and social science, the Center studies the regulatory, financial, and market forces shaping housing production, access, and affordability. Through policy evaluation, comparative research, and practical analysis, we provide policymakers, practitioners, and community leaders with the evidence they need to design more effective housing solutions and build communities with a full continuum of housing opportunities.
