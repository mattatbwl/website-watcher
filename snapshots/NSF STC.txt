About
The NSF STC program supports large-scale, sustained collaborations that enable scientific and technological advances that can only be achieved through integrated research. STCs accomplish these goals by building strategic partnerships among institutions of higher education, along with government laboratories, industry, nonprofit organizations and other entities, as appropriate. Through these partnerships, STCs seek to create new scientific models, develop transformative technologies and generate broad impacts on science and society.
STC is an agencywide program that supports research in areas that advance NSF's mission. Each STC is designed to fulfill a unique vision at the leading edge of science and engineering using novel, highly creative approaches within and across disciplines. They also advance education by combining research with learning and developing new ways of training the future workforce. By creating environments that encourage collaboration, creativity and risk-taking, the program aims to accelerate breakthroughs at the frontiers of current knowledge and empower future generations.
History
The STC program was established by the National Science Board in 1987, and the first class of STCs was established in 1989. Since its inception, the STC program has catalyzed the emergence of new fields of discovery, advanced breakthrough technologies and trained generations of researchers and innovators.
Over its history, the STC portfolio has invested in creating new scientific and technological models in a wide variety of topics across NSF areas of research. Together, the active and graduated centers demonstrate the program's long-standing investment, as indicated in the links below.

Thursday, September 19, 2024
Science and Technology Centers Informational Webinar
Credit: U.S. National Science Foundation
The NSF STC program team presented a webinar about the STC program, with an emphasis on key leadership considerations for potential STC Principal Investigators / future Center Directors. Topics included highlights from the STC solicitation, a presentation on common challenges experienced by Center Directors and a panel discussion with current STC Directors, followed by a Q&A session.
