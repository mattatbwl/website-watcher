JETC 2027: Where Innovation Meets Impact
Advancing a Shared Commitment. Forging a Stronger Future.
From Policy to Practice. From Partnerships to Projects.
Join military engineers, government leaders, industry experts, and innovators at the premier event advancing engineering solutions for national security, infrastructure resilience, and mission success.
For more than a century, SAME has connected professionals across the military, government, industry, and academic communities to solve challenges, strengthen partnerships, and support the nation's most critical missions. JETC 2027 continues that tradition by bringing together the people, ideas, and technologies shaping the future of engineering and the built environment.
Whether you're seeking technical education, professional development, new partnerships, acquisition insights, or emerging technologies, JETC offers unmatched opportunities to connect with the leaders and solutions making an impact today and preparing for tomorrow.
