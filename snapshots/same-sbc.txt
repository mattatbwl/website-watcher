Learn more about SBC 2026
SAME’s 2026 Federal Small Business Conference (SBC) is headed to
Charlotte, NC
, November 4-6, 2026. This is your opportunity to connect with a community of leading experts, decision-makers, solution providers, business partners, and federal contracting officers.
Join us at the 2026 SBC — and fuel the future of faster, smarter federal acquisition
SBC 2026 is
The Strategic Business Connection of the Year
—the place where opportunity meets mission and where the federal A/E/C community unites to
strengthen partnerships and drive innovation. If you are shaping the future of federal programs, you belong in Charlotte this November.
Building on the momentum of SBC 2025—where over 5,100 professionals united to advance mission focused solutions—SBC 2026 returns bigger and more impactful, bringing together thousands of federal and industry leaders committed to shaping the future of national security infrastructure.
