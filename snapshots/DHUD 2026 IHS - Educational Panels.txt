WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2026 - THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026
VIRTUAL AND IN PERSON
On September 23–24, 2026, HUD will host a series of hybrid (in person and virtual) panels on innovative housing. These educational panels are in conjunction with HUD's annual
Innovative Housing Showcase
, a public event to raise awareness of innovative and affordable housing designs and technologies.
More information to come – check back soon!
Wednesday, September 23
Capital, Construction, Community: Opportunity Zones at Work
12:00 - 12:45 pm
Technological Innovation in Real Estate Markets
1:00 - 1:45 pm
Thursday, September 24
Financing the Future of Housing
1:00 - 1:45 pm
Made in America: From Policy to Practice
2:00 - 2:45 pm
Register
Members of the press should register to attend the Innovative Housing Showcase
here
.
