How to become a listed Ohio IU manufacturer
Why we regulate
The Board of Building Standards regulates factory-built construction components (excluding those covered by Ohio's Manufactured Homes Program). Our goal is to keep the process simple, speed plan review, and ensure code-compliant products for Ohio homeowners and businesses.
What we do
Review and approve construction documents for factory-built, closed construction.
Certify factory inspectors who perform in-plant inspections.
Sell insignias that show units are approved for use in Ohio.
How to register
To start, email us at E.plans@com.state.oh.us with the following information for each manufacturer or assembler whose products will be used in Ohio:
Manufacturer or assembler name
Facility address(es)
where the construction or assembly will occur. If you have multiple facilities that produce products for Ohio, list each address. Also provide your corporate headquarters address and phone number.
Primary contact
for construction documents: name, email, and phone number.
Inspection entity
that will perform in-plant inspections (third-party inspection agency, a certified Ohio building department, or the state for periodic audits). After initial registration, provide signed letters from both the manufacturer and the inspection agency confirming their inspection agreement.
What you receive
We will email you a Manufacturer User ID and password. Use these credentials to access Ohio's online Industrialized Unit system.
Fees and using the online system
Initial application and plan review:
$135. This reserves one hour of plan review time and must be paid before your documents are placed in the review queue. Many typical home and commercial modular plans are approved within this hour if referenced system documents are already on file or your submittal is complete and detailed.
Additional plan review:
$100 per hour, billed in ¬Ω-hour increments. If the review requires more time, we will notify you of the balance due after approval and send an invoice by email. You must pay the invoice before approved documents can be released or downloaded.
Insignias:
Modular unit insignia: $50 each (required for each preassembled modular unit).
Panelized component insignia: $1 per 20 sq. ft. of wall, floor, or roof area (required for panelized components shipped into Ohio).
Manufacturers request and purchase insignias electronically through the IU portal.
